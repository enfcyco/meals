Family Hamburger Buns

A quick, soft homemade bun sized for four. Our batch is especially good alongside pulled pork.

Yield: 4 buns Bake: about 9½ minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 packet active dry yeast Family note: We use one packet; the source recipe scaled to half makes for 1 tablespoon.
  • ½ cup warm water
  • ⅙ cup cooking oil (2 tablespoons + 2 teaspoons)
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • ½ large egg, beaten (beat one egg and use half, about 1½ tablespoons)
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1½ to 1¾ cups bread flour or all-purpose flour

Directions

  1. In a bowl, dissolve the yeast in the warm water. Stir in the oil and sugar, then leave the mixture for 5 minutes.
  2. Mix in half of the beaten egg, the salt, and 1½ cups flour. Add more flour a little at a time until a soft dough forms.
  3. Turn the dough onto the counter or work in the bowl. Use a dough scraper to knead for a few minutes, until the dough is smooth. Family note: We use a scraper rather than a mixer.
  4. Divide the dough into four pieces and shape into buns. Cover and let rise for about 20 minutes before turning on the oven; while it heats, the total rise is about 25 minutes. Family note: Our rise starts before the oven is turned on.
  5. Heat the oven to 410°F. Bake the buns for about 9½ minutes, until set and lightly golden. Cool before serving.

Family note: “Flavor is low, but that’s good for pulled pork.”

Source: The Feathered Nester, “Quick and Easy Hamburger Bun Recipe,” by Renae Gerhardstein (updated 04/19/2024). This is our half-batch family version.
