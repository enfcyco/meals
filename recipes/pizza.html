Weeknight Pizza

A crisp, cheesy homemade pizza with a quick tomato sauce. Use your favorite toppings or keep it classic.

Prep: 20 min Cook: 15 min Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound pizza dough, store-bought or homemade
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Your favorite toppings, such as pepperoni or sliced vegetables
  • Dried oregano and grated Parmesan, to finish

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 475°F. If using a pizza stone, let it heat in the oven.
  2. Stretch the dough on a lightly floured surface. Move it to a baking sheet or parchment.
  3. Spread the sauce over the dough, leaving a small border. Add mozzarella and your toppings.
  4. Bake for 12–15 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
  5. Finish with oregano and Parmesan. Rest for a few minutes, slice, and serve.

Recipe starter: change the toppings, amounts, and notes to match the version your family loves.
