Family Taco Night

Seasoned beef, warm tortillas, and all the toppings at the table so everyone can build their own.

Prep: 10 min Cook: 15 min Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 8 small tortillas
  • Toppings: shredded lettuce, cheese, salsa, and sour cream

Directions

  1. Warm the oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the beef and cook, breaking it up, until browned.
  2. Drain excess fat if needed. Stir in the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, and water.
  3. Simmer for 5 minutes, stirring now and then, until the sauce thickens.
  4. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave.
  5. Serve the beef with tortillas and toppings so everyone can make their own tacos.
