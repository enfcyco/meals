Chicken Tortilla Soup III

A creamy chicken soup with salsa and corn, served over tortilla chips with Monterey Jack cheese.

Serves: 8

Ingredients

  • 3 cloves garlic, minced Handwritten family modification: use 2 cloves.
  • 1 onion, chopped Handwritten family modification: use 1/2 onion.
  • 3 tablespoons margarine Handwritten family modification: use 2 tablespoons.
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour Handwritten family modification: use 1 tablespoon.
  • 3 (14-ounce) cans chicken broth Handwritten family modification: use 1 1/2 cans.
  • 4 cups half-and-half Handwritten family modification: use 2 cups.
  • 1 (10.75-ounce) can condensed cream of chicken soup Handwritten family modification: use 1/2 can.
  • 1 cup fresh salsa Handwritten family modification: use 1/2 cup.
  • 1 (15-ounce) can creamed corn Handwritten family modification: use 1/2 can.
  • 6 boneless chicken breast halves, cooked and skinned Handwritten family modification: use 3 chicken breast halves.
  • 2 teaspoons ground cumin Handwritten family modification: use 1 teaspoon.
  • 1 (1.27-ounce) packet dry fajita seasoning Handwritten family modification: use 1/2 packet.
  • 3 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro Handwritten family modification: use 2 tablespoons total.
  • 16 ounces tortilla chips
  • 8 ounces shredded Monterey Jack cheese

Directions

  1. In a large pot over medium heat, cook the garlic and onion in the margarine for 5 minutes. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  2. Add the chicken broth and half-and-half. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat.
  3. Stir in the cream of chicken soup, salsa, creamed corn, cooked chicken, cumin, fajita seasoning, and 2 tablespoons cilantro. Heat for 15 minutes. The printed directions reserve the remaining 1 tablespoon cilantro for serving. Handwritten family modification: With 2 tablespoons total, use 1 tablespoon in the soup and save the other tablespoon for garnish.
  4. Serve the soup over tortilla chips and shredded cheese. Top each bowl with more chips and cheese and the remaining cilantro.
