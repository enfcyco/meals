Family favorite

Tim's Dad's Beans

A hearty mix of beans, bacon, and hamburger in a sweet and tangy sauce, warmed in the crockpot.

Ingredients

  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • 1 can lima beans, drained
  • 1 can butter beans, drained
  • 1 can kidney beans, drained
  • 1 can Bush's beans, undrained
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup barbecue sauce
  • 1 cup Western dressing
  • 2 tablespoons mustard
  • 1 pound bacon
  • 1 pound hamburger (ground beef)
  • 1 onion, cooked

Directions

  1. Drain the black, lima, butter, and kidney beans. Do not drain the Bush's beans.
  2. Cook the bacon and hamburger thoroughly in a skillet. Drain excess grease. Cook the onion until softened.
  3. Add all the beans, brown sugar, barbecue sauce, Western dressing, mustard, cooked bacon, hamburger, and onion to a crockpot. Stir to combine.
  4. Cover and warm on low until hot throughout, stirring occasionally.

Transcribed from a handwritten family recipe. Bean can sizes and crockpot warming time were not specified.
