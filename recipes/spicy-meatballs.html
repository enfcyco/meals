Spicy Meatballs

Frozen Italian meatballs warmed in a sweet, savory, and lightly spicy sauce.

Yield: 4–6 servings Prep: 10 minutes Total: 2–4 hours

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup rice vinegar
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup chili garlic sauce (Huy Fong)
  • 1 tablespoon garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
  • 1 splash sesame seed oil
  • 1 bag frozen Italian meatballs

Directions

  1. Whisk together the soy sauce, rice vinegar, honey, chili garlic sauce, garlic, ginger, and sesame oil in the crock pot.
  2. Add the frozen Italian meatballs and toss until they are coated in the sauce.
  3. Cover and cook on low until the meatballs are fully warmed through and the sauce is glossy, about 2–4 hours.
  4. Serve as-is or over rice with a side of vegetables.
