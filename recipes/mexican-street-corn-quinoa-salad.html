Mexican Street Corn Quinoa Salad

Charred corn, quinoa, dairy-free feta, and a smoky lime dressing make a picnic-ready salad.

Serves: 6–8 Total: 30 minutes (15 prep, 15 cook)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup uncooked quinoa
  • 2 cups water
  • 1–2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 (15-ounce) cans corn, drained (or fresh or frozen corn)
  • ⅓ cup diced red onion
  • 1 cup dairy-free feta cheese, divided
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh cilantro, plus leaves for garnish

Smoky lime dressing

  • ¼ cup mayonnaise
  • ½ tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice, plus more if desired
  • ½ teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper

Directions

  1. Cook the quinoa in the water according to package directions, about 15 minutes, until the water is absorbed.
  2. Meanwhile, warm the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the corn and cook until lightly charred, about 7–10 minutes for canned or fresh corn; frozen corn may take longer.
  3. Whisk the dressing ingredients together until smooth.
  4. Combine the quinoa, corn, red onion, and half the feta in a large bowl. Add the dressing and toss to coat. Let cool for 10–15 minutes, then fold in the remaining feta and cilantro.
  5. Garnish with cilantro and extra lime juice if desired. Serve at room temperature.

Note: Used Walden Farms Amazin Mayo, found at Fareway.

Source: “Dairy-Free Mexican Street Corn Quinoa Salad,” Gina Fontana, Healthy Little Vittles, July 2, 2022.
