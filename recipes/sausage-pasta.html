One-Pan Cheesy Smoked Sausage & Pasta

Smoked sausage, pasta, and tomatoes cook together in a creamy, cheesy one-pan meal.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 pound smoked sausage
  • 1/2 cup diced onion
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 1 can (14 ounces) diced tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 8 ounces dry pasta
  • 1/2 teaspoon each salt and pepper
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar-jack cheese
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1/2 cup chopped scallions, for garnish

Directions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the onion and sausage and cook for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes; cook for 2 minutes.
  3. Stir in the chicken broth, tomatoes, milk, and pasta. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat, cover, and cook for 15 minutes.
  4. Stir in the cheese. Serve garnished with scallions.
