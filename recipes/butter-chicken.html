Famous Butter Chicken

Chicken breasts coated in seasoned cracker crumbs and baked with butter until tender.

Prep: 15 min Cook: 40 min Total: 55 min Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup crushed buttery round cracker crumbs
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic salt
  • Ground black pepper, to taste
  • 4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • 1/2 cup butter, cut into pieces

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Put the beaten eggs and cracker crumbs in separate shallow bowls. Season the crumbs with garlic salt and pepper.
  3. Dip each piece of chicken in egg, then coat it in the crumbs. Arrange the chicken in a 9-by-13-inch baking dish and place the butter pieces around it.
  4. Bake for about 40 minutes, until the chicken is no longer pink inside and its juices run clear.
