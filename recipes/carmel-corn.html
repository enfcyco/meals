Carmel Corn

Warm, buttery popcorn coated in a glossy caramel shell with a crisp, crunchy finish.

Yield: 10 cups Prep: 10 min Cook: 45 min

Ingredients

  • 10 cups popped popcorn
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup light corn syrup
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 250°F and line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper or foil.
  2. Place the popped popcorn in a large bowl and set it aside.
  3. In a saucepan over medium heat, combine the brown sugar, butter, corn syrup, and salt. Stir until the butter melts and the mixture comes to a gentle boil.
  4. Let the caramel boil without stirring for 3 to 4 minutes, until it is glossy and slightly thickened.
  5. Remove from the heat and stir in the baking soda and vanilla. The mixture will foam a little and become lighter.
  6. Quickly pour the caramel over the popcorn and toss gently to coat as evenly as possible.
  7. Spread the coated popcorn on the prepared baking sheet in a thin layer.
  8. Bake for 30 minutes, stirring once or twice to keep it from clumping. If you want it extra crisp, bake another 10 to 15 minutes.
  9. Let it cool completely, then break it into clusters and serve.

Recipe starter: add a pinch of cinnamon or a handful of peanuts if you want a little extra flavor and crunch.
