Family favorite

Cozy Lasagna

Layers of pasta, a hearty tomato and meat sauce, and a cheesy filling make this a favorite for sharing.

Prep: 25 min Cook: 50 min Serves: 6–8

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 jar (about 24 ounces) marinara sauce
  • 1 egg
  • 15 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella, divided
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Cook the noodles according to the package directions; drain.
  2. Brown the beef in a skillet. Drain excess fat, stir in the marinara, and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. In a bowl, mix the egg, ricotta, 1 cup mozzarella, Parmesan, and Italian seasoning.
  4. Spread a little sauce in a 9-by-13-inch baking dish. Layer noodles, ricotta mixture, and sauce; repeat, finishing with sauce and the remaining mozzarella.
  5. Cover with foil and bake for 35 minutes. Uncover and bake 15 minutes more, until bubbling.
  6. Rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
