Carrot Cake with Candied Pecans

Moist carrot cake with pineapple and coconut, layered with tangy cream cheese frosting, crunchy candied pecans, and brown sugar butterscotch.

Yield: 16 servings Prep: 2 hours 30 minutes Cook: 1 hour 10 minutes

Adapted from The Pancake Princess. The cake was formerly published in Southern Living.

Ingredients

For the carrot cake

  • 2 cups granulated sugar (400 g)
  • 1½ cups neutral oil, such as vegetable, canola, or grapeseed (300 g)
  • 4 large eggs (200 g)
  • 1 tablespoon cinnamon
  • 1½ teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ½ teaspoon ginger
  • ¼ teaspoon cloves
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (240 g)
  • 3 cups finely grated carrots (297 g)
  • ¾ cup diced pecans, toasted if possible (85 g)
  • Scant ½ cup crushed pineapple, partially drained (113 g / 4 oz)
  • ¾ cup sweetened flaked or shredded coconut (62 g)

For the candied pecans

  • 1 large egg white (30–35 g), reserving the yolk for the butterscotch
  • 2 cups diced pecans (228 g)
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar (50 g)
  • ¼ cup light or dark brown sugar (57 g)
  • 2 teaspoons cinnamon
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt

Family shortcut: Use the pecan crumbs at the bottom of the Sam’s Club pecan container for the candied pecans.

For the butterscotch

  • 1 cup light or dark brown sugar (213 g)
  • ½ cup heavy cream (113 g)
  • 1 large egg yolk (14 g)
  • 1 tablespoon corn syrup, optional
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter (14 g)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of kosher or flaky salt

For the cream cheese frosting

  • 8 oz cream cheese, cool (226 g)
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, at room temperature (113 g)
  • 3 cups powdered sugar (339 g)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of kosher salt

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Line a 13-by-18-inch half-sheet pan with parchment, creasing it to fit.
  2. Whisk the sugar, oil, eggs, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and spices together. Whisk in the flour, then fold in the carrots, pecans, pineapple, and coconut. Spread the batter in the pan and bake for 28–33 minutes, until a tester comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs. Cool completely in the pan.
  3. For the candied pecans, heat the oven to 300°F and line a baking sheet with parchment. Whisk the egg white until slightly foamy, then toss it with the pecans. Mix in both sugars, the cinnamon, and salt until coated. Spread on the sheet and bake for 25–40 minutes, stirring after 20 minutes. Cool completely; the pecans crisp as they cool.
  4. For the butterscotch, whisk the egg yolk, cream, and brown sugar in a small saucepan. Stir in the optional corn syrup. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until boiling; continue for 2–3 minutes, until thickened and bubbling. Remove from the heat and stir in the butter, vanilla, and salt. Cool completely.
  5. For the frosting, beat the cream cheese and butter just until smooth. Gradually beat in the powdered sugar, then the vanilla and salt. Beat on medium-high for 1–2 minutes, until pale and fluffy.
  6. Cut the cooled cake in half lengthwise, then crosswise to make four pieces. Layer the cake pieces with frosting, candied pecans, and butterscotch between each layer. Leave the sides unfrosted for a rustic finish, if desired. Serve with any remaining butterscotch and pecans.
