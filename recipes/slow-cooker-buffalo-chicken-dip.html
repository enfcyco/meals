Slow Cooker Buffalo Chicken Dip

A creamy, spicy chicken dip that comes together in one slow cooker and is perfect for sharing with chips, veggies, or crackers.

Prep: 5 min Cook: 2 hr Serves: 64

Ingredients

  • 2 packages (8 ounces each) cream cheese, cut into chunks
  • 4 cups shredded cooked chicken
  • 1 cup Frank's RedHot® Original Cayenne Pepper Hot Sauce
  • 1 cup ranch dressing
  • 1 cup shredded Cheddar cheese, divided
  • 2 tablespoons chopped green onions

Directions

  1. Place the cream cheese, shredded chicken, hot sauce, ranch dressing, and 3/4 cup of the cheddar in a slow cooker.
  2. Stir gently to combine, then cover and cook on low for 2 hours, stirring once or twice if needed.
  3. When the dip is hot and creamy, sprinkle with the remaining cheddar and green onions.
  4. Serve warm with tortilla chips, celery, carrots, or crackers.

This version keeps things easy: everything goes in the slow cooker and you can let it do the work while you enjoy the game.
