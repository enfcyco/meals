Crab Rangoon Pizza

Creamy crab, green onions, and melted cheese on a crisp thin crust, finished with crunchy wonton crisps and sweet chili sauce.

Prep: 10 min Cook: About 15 min Serves: 4–6

Ingredients

  • 1 precooked thin-crust pizza crust
  • About 6 oz imitation crab (most of an 8 oz package), chopped or shredded
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 6 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 6 tablespoons sliced green onions, divided
  • 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese, plus a little more for topping
  • 4 oz shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
  • About ½ cup wonton crisps, crumbled (the crunchy topping sold near the croutons)
  • 2 tablespoons sweet chili sauce, or to taste

Directions

  1. Heat the oven and prepare the crust according to its package directions.
  2. Stir together the cream cheese and diced onion until smooth.
  3. In a separate bowl, mix the imitation crab with 2 tablespoons of the sliced green onions and 2 tablespoons of the mozzarella. Spread the cream cheese mixture over the crust. Top with the crab mixture, then sprinkle with the remaining mozzarella, the Parmesan, the remaining green onions, and the crumbled wonton crisps.
  4. Bake according to the crust package directions, until the cheese is melted and the edges are hot and lightly browned, about 8–10 minutes. Avoid overbaking the precooked crust.
  5. Drizzle with sweet chili sauce, slice, and serve.

The packaged wonton crisps are an easy stand-in for frying wonton strips—just crumble them over the pizza before baking. Check the crust and crisp packages if you need this to be gluten-free.
