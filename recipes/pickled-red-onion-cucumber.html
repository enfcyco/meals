Quick refrigerator pickle

Pickled Red Onion & Cucumber

Crisp red onion and cucumber in a tangy red-wine-vinegar brine with garlic, bay, and peppercorns.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups red wine vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 teaspoons peppercorns
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 whole red onion, cut into pieces
  • 1 cucumber, cut into pieces

Directions

  1. Combine the red wine vinegar, water, bay leaves, peppercorns, sugar, salt, and garlic in a saucepan. Bring the brine to a boil, stirring until the sugar and salt dissolve.
  2. Remove the pan from the heat. Add the cut red onion and cucumber to the hot brine and stir to coat.
  3. Transfer the vegetables and brine to a clean, heat-safe glass canning jar. Handle the hot brine and jar carefully.
  4. Let the mixture cool in the jar, then cover and refrigerate.

Refrigerator pickles only; this recipe is not a tested method for shelf-stable canning. Keep refrigerated.
