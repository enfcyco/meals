Classic celebration cake

Tres Leches Cake

A tender sponge cake soaked with three kinds of milk, chilled, and finished with fresh whipped cream and cinnamon.

Adapted from Tastes Better From Scratch.

Ingredients

For the cake

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1½ teaspoons baking powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 5 large eggs, separated
  • 1 cup granulated sugar, divided
  • ⅓ cup whole milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

For the milk soak

  • 1 (12-ounce) can evaporated milk
  • 1 (14-ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
  • ¼ cup whole milk

For the topping

  • 1 pint heavy whipping cream
  • 3 tablespoons granulated or powdered sugar
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Ground cinnamon, for sprinkling
  • Fresh sliced strawberries, optional

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease a 9-by-13-inch baking pan.
  2. Whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt. In a separate bowl, beat the egg yolks with ¾ cup of the sugar until pale and thick. Mix in the ⅓ cup milk and vanilla, then fold the yolk mixture into the dry ingredients just until combined.
  3. In a clean bowl, beat the egg whites. Gradually add the remaining ¼ cup sugar and continue beating until stiff peaks form. Fold the whites gently into the batter until no large streaks remain.
  4. Spread the batter evenly in the pan. Bake for 25–35 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool the cake completely.
  5. Whisk the evaporated milk, condensed milk, and ¼ cup whole milk together. Pierce the cooled cake all over with a fork about 100 times or more, making sure the milk gets into those holes. Slowly pour the milk mixture over it, including around the edges, but don’t feel like you need to use all of it—too much can make it soggy.
  6. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or preferably overnight, so the cake can absorb the milk.
  7. Beat the cream, sugar, and vanilla until stiff peaks form. Spread over the chilled cake and sprinkle with cinnamon. Add strawberries if you like, then serve cold.
