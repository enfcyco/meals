Caramel Sticky Rolls

Soft cinnamon rolls baked over a buttery caramel and pecan topping.

Yield: 15 rolls Prep: 40 minutes Total: 3 hours 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 3½ to 4 cups all-purpose or bread flour
  • ⅓ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 packages (4½ teaspoons) active dry or fast-acting dry yeast
  • 1 cup very warm milk (120–130°F)
  • ¼ cup butter, softened
  • 1 egg

Caramel topping

  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • ½ cup butter, softened
  • ¼ cup light corn syrup
  • 1 cup pecan halves, optional

Filling

  • ½ cup chopped pecans or raisins, optional
  • ¼ cup granulated or packed brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 2 tablespoons butter, softened

Directions

  1. Warm the milk to 120–130°F. Mix the dough ingredients, adding the flour gradually to form a soft dough.
  2. Knead the dough and let it rise until doubled, about 1½ hours.
  3. In a saucepan, heat the topping's brown sugar and ½ cup butter to boiling, stirring occasionally. Remove from the heat and stir in the corn syrup. Pour into an ungreased 13-by-9-inch pan and scatter pecan halves over it, if using.
  4. Combine the filling sugar and cinnamon with the chopped pecans or raisins, if using. Punch down the dough and roll it into a rectangle. Spread with the softened butter, then sprinkle on the filling. Starting at a long edge, roll up the dough and cut into 15 slices.

    Our version: Roll the dough out on a large wooden cutting board. Spread Blue Bonnet Lite over the dough, then sprinkle with brown sugar and cinnamon before rolling it up. This family method is a variation on the printed filling directions.

  5. Arrange the slices in the pan over the caramel. Cover and let rise until doubled, about 30 minutes.
  6. Heat the oven to 350°F. Bake for 30–35 minutes, until golden. Let stand for 2–3 minutes, then invert onto a serving tray to release the caramel.
