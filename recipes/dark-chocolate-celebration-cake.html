Dark Chocolate Celebration Cake

A rich chocolate layer cake covered in glossy chocolate ganache and finished with an elegant piped chocolate border.

Yield: 10 to 12 slices Prep: 45 minutes Cook: 30 to 35 minutes

Ingredients

For the cake

  • 1¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon fine salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 cup hot brewed coffee or hot water

For the ganache

  • 12 ounces semisweet or dark chocolate, finely chopped
  • 1½ cups heavy cream
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened

For decorating

  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • 4 ounces semisweet chocolate, finely chopped

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease two 9-inch round cake pans and line the bottoms with parchment.
  2. Whisk the flour, cocoa, sugar, baking soda, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl. Add the eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla; beat until smooth, then slowly whisk in the hot coffee.
  3. Divide the batter evenly between the pans. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out with a few moist crumbs. Cool for 15 minutes, turn out onto racks, and cool completely.
  4. For the ganache, place the chopped chocolate in a heatproof bowl. Heat the cream just until steaming, pour it over the chocolate, and let stand for 3 minutes. Stir until smooth, then mix in the butter.
  5. Cool the ganache until thick enough to spread, stirring occasionally. Place one cake layer on a serving plate, spread with a thin layer of ganache, and top with the second layer.
  6. Spread the remaining ganache over the top and sides, smoothing the sides and letting a little gloss remain on the surface.
  7. For the piped border, warm ½ cup cream until steaming and pour it over 4 ounces chopped chocolate. Stir smooth and cool until pipeable. Transfer to a piping bag and pipe a decorative ruffle or shell border around the top edge.
  8. Let the cake stand until the ganache sets slightly, then slice and serve.
