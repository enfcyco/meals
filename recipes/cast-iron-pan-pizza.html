Cast Iron Pan Pizza

A long-rested, airy dough bakes up crisp in a cast-iron skillet under tomato sauce and two cheeses.

Time: 1¼ hr + 14 hr resting Serves: 4

Dough

  • 2 cups (11 oz) bread flour
  • 1 teaspoon table salt
  • 1 teaspoon instant or rapid-rise yeast
  • 1 cup (8 oz) warm water (105–110°F)
  • Vegetable oil spray

Sauce

  • 1 can (14.5 oz) whole peeled tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • ¼ teaspoon sugar
  • ¼ teaspoon table salt
  • ¼ teaspoon dried oregano
  • Pinch of red pepper flakes

Pizza

  • 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 oz (1 cup) shredded Monterey Jack
  • 7 oz (1¾ cups) shredded whole-milk mozzarella

Directions

  1. Stir together the flour, salt, and yeast, then add the warm water and mix into a rough dough. Knead briefly until cohesive, shape into a ball, and refrigerate in a covered, oiled container for 12–24 hours.
  2. Drain the tomatoes, then blend them with the olive oil, garlic, sugar, salt, oregano, and red pepper flakes until smooth.
  3. Let the dough come to room temperature. Oil a 12-inch cast-iron skillet, set the dough inside, and stretch it toward the edges. Cover and leave it until puffy.
  4. Heat the oven to 400°F. Spread sauce over the dough, then add the mozzarella and Monterey Jack, taking some Jack all the way to the edge of the pan.
  5. Bake until the crust is browned and the cheese is bubbling. Briefly cook the skillet on the stovetop to crisp the bottom, then cool a few minutes before slicing.

A 12-inch cast-iron skillet works best. For the cheese, start with blocks rather than fresh or pre-shredded cheese; weighing the flour and water helps keep the dough consistent.
